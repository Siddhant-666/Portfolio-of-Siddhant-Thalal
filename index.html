Hyper-Redundant Aerial Manipulator — Rope-Space Modeling & Experimental Validation 🎥

Designed and analytically modeled a tendon-driven hyper-redundant aerial manipulator enabling large spherical workspace coverage while decoupling the UAV base from the interaction region. Derived a recursive kinematic formulation for an N-link prismatic–universal chain and established a rope-space mapping between tendon length variations and end-effector pose.

Built an experimental validation pipeline integrating encoder-based tendon measurements with motion capture ground truth for joint-space and task-space error analysis. Demonstrated accurate pose realization and validated spherical workspace predictions through closed-loop experiments.

Tech: Continuum Robotics, Tendon-Driven Systems, Kinematics, MATLAB, Motion Capture (OptiTrack), Embedded Systems

XR-Based Remote Teleoperation & Digital Twin Development 🎥

Developed an immersive XR teleoperation framework enabling real-time 3D visualization and intuitive spatial control of a robotic system. Built a URDF-based digital twin for accurate kinematic replication and synchronized it with live robot telemetry.

Implemented a WebSocket-based bidirectional communication layer enabling low-latency global remote control, allowing the robot to be operated remotely from any location. Demonstrated spatial interaction tasks (target touching) through embodied XR interfaces, establishing a foundation for future gripper-based manipulation and shared autonomy.

Tech: Human–Robot Interaction, XR/VR Interfaces, URDF, WebSockets, Real-Time Systems, Robot Control

Featured Projects

Camel-Inspired Quadruped Foot for Locomotion on Granular Terrain

Challenge: Conventional robotic feet sink and lose traction on sand.

Developed a biomimetic expanding foot inspired by camel morphology to improve quadruped stability and mobility on deformable sand. Designed and simulated the mechanism (IK + dynamics), performed FEA stress analysis, and validated performance through sandbox experiments using FSR sensing and ArUco-based trajectory tracking.

Skills: CAD, Mechanism Design, Terramechanics, Multibody Simulation, Inverse Kinematics, MATLAB/Simulink (Simscape), Control Systems, FEA, Sensing & Experimental Validation

Patent Filed: Design submitted for IP protection

Buoyancy-Based Depth Control for an Underwater Robot

Problem: Underwater robots lose stability due to drag and poor buoyancy control.

Designed and implemented a buoyancy-regulated depth control module using a syringe– based water intake/release system. Built a sealed Arduino-powered unit with a pressure sensor, motor driver, syringe actuator, and Bluetooth telemetry. Implemented P and PID control loops to achieve stable depth-holding under disturbances, validated through simulations (Simulink) and experiments.

Skills: Embedded Control, Buoyancy Dynamics, Sensors, MATLAB/Simulink, PID, Prototyping
